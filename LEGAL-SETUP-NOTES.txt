Before the final commercial launch
1. Confirm the proprietor's full legal name or registered company name.
2. Confirm the full postal/trading address shown on booking confirmations and invoices.
3. Add a dedicated privacy/contact email if one is available.
4. Confirm that the stated retention periods match the business's real deletion process.
5. List the actual payment, hosting, file-service and communication providers used.
6. If analytics, advertising pixels or non-essential cookies are added, install compliant consent controls and update the privacy policy before activation.
7. Use a written request/acknowledgement when a consumer asks for work to begin during the 14-day cancellation period.
8. Keep the road-use and emissions wording aligned with the services actually offered. Do not advertise DPF removal or emissions defeat for road vehicles.
9. Have a UK solicitor review the final commercial version once the legal entity, address, booking, deposit and warranty model are confirmed.

These pages are tailored operational drafts. They are not a substitute for legal advice.
